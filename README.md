# Astrology Chart Calculator

A free, standalone Chinese Astrology chart app that generates your **Zi Wei Dou Shu (ZWDS)** and **BaZi (Eight Characters)** birth chart — no install required, just open the HTML file in your browser.

Built by **Andy Low**.

---

## What it does

- Generates a full ZWDS 12-palace natal chart and BaZi Four Pillars chart from your name, gender, date and time of birth
- Supports Major Luck Cycle (大运) and Annual Luck (流年) navigation
- Exports your chart as a text file ready to submit to an AI for a reading
- Saves your charts automatically in your browser — no account needed
- Available in English and Chinese (中文)

---

## How to use

1. Download `destiny_reading.html` and open it in Chrome or Edge
2. Click **+ Add** and enter your name, gender, date and time of birth
3. Your chart appears instantly
4. Click **Export** to download your chart as a .txt file
5. Attach the file to an AI chat (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT) and ask for a reading

See **User Manual.txt** for full instructions and suggested AI prompts.

## Time of birth note

Enter the **local clock time** at the place of birth. Do not adjust for daylight saving time.
