GETTING STARTED
---------------
Open destiny_reading.html in your web browser (Chrome or Edge recommended).
If you want a Desktop shortcut, run "Create Desktop Shortcut.bat" once.


ADDING YOUR BIRTH CHART
------------------------
1. Click "+ Add" in the top bar.

2. Fill in the form:
   - Name        : Your name or any label you like
   - Gender      : Male or Female
   - Date of Birth : Your birth date (year / month / day)
   - Time of Birth : The time you were born

   IMPORTANT — Time of Birth:
   Enter the LOCAL time at the place you were born.
   Do NOT adjust for daylight saving time.
   If you were born in London in summer, and the clock said 3:00 PM,
   enter 15:00 — even though clocks were on BST at the time.
   Use the raw clock time of the country/city of birth.

   If you do not know your birth time, you can go create all 12 different charts for that day
   and ask the AI to help you find which hour you were born in by giving it more information about you.

3. Click "Register". Your chart will appear immediately.


SWITCHING BETWEEN CHARTS
-------------------------
Use the dropdown in the top bar to switch between saved people.
You can add as many people as you like.


EXPORTING YOUR CHART FOR AI ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------
1. Select the person whose chart you want to export.
2. Click "Export" in the top bar.
3. A .txt file will download to your computer.
   This file contains your full BaZi (Eight Characters) and
   Zi Wei Dou Shu (ZWDS) chart data in Chinese.


SUBMITTING TO AI FOR YOUR READING
-----------------------------------
Attach the exported .txt file along with your prompts into
an AI chat (such as Claude or Gemini). Example prompts are:


--- PROMPT: PERSONALITY & DESTINY READING ---

Attached is my Chinese Astrology birth data. Using both Bazi and ZWDS together, please give me a reading covering:
- My core personality and natural strengths
- Career and wealth potential
- Relationships and marriage

TIPS
----
- The app saves your charts automatically in your browser.
  You do not need to re-enter them each time you open the app.
- To back up your charts, click "↓ JSON" to save all your data.
  To restore them on another device, click "↑ JSON" and load the file.
- Use the EN / 中文 toggle in the top bar to switch languages.
- Use the − / + buttons to adjust text size if the chart feels cramped.
